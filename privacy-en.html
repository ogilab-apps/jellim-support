Privacy Policy

Effective: October 6, 2026

Jellim does not collect personal information such as your name or email address. The jellies you add and the times you ease your stress are stored only on your device and are never transmitted outside your device. Information needed to verify a purchase is processed only when you use in-app purchases (see “6. In-App Purchases”).

1. Developer

Jellim (“the App”) is developed and operated by an independent developer (“we”) as an app for keeping track of stress.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location. No account is required.

Everything you record in the App — the kind, size, and time of each jelly you add, the times you eased and what helped, the state of the jellies in your cup, and your settings — is stored only in a database on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party. The App does not send crash reports or other diagnostic information.

3. Device Motion

The App uses your device's motion (acceleration) to make the jellies in the cup wobble when you shake your iPhone. This information is used only on your device to move the jellies and is neither stored nor transmitted.

You can turn this off at any time with “Shake to wobble” in the App's Settings.

4. Sharing a Picture of Your Cup

The share feature makes a picture on your device of your cup as it is now, with how full it is, the color breakdown and, if you choose, the names of the kinds of stress. The picture is handed to the iOS share sheet, and you choose where to send or save it. How the picture is handled there is governed by the terms of each service.

5. Saving to Photos

Only when you choose to save from the share sheet, the App uses permission to add the picture of your cup to Photos. The App never reads the contents of your photo library. You can change this permission at any time in iOS Settings.

6. In-App Purchases

Purchases of Plus are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never have access to your payment details, such as credit card information.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to verify purchase status (receipt validation) and analyze purchase history. In this process, purchase history, receipt information, and an anonymous identifier are handled on RevenueCat's servers. This information is not linked to personal information such as your name or email address, and is not used for tracking across other apps or websites.

For details, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

7. External Transmission and Third Parties

  • Your records are never sent to an external server. The App works offline.
  • Except for the purchase processing described above, we do not share your information with third parties.
  • We do not use app-usage analytics tools or advertising services.
  • The only network communication the App performs is related to in-app purchases, as described above.

8. Backups

The App's data is stored on your device and is included in standard iOS backups (iCloud or computer). Restoring a new device from such a backup brings your records with it. These backups are made under your Apple Account, and we have no access to their contents.

9. Deleting Your Data

The App's data is stored only on your device, so deleting the App deletes all of it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when laws change or features are added. Important changes will be announced on this page.
